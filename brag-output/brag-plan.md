# Brag Plan: Bridge

## What is this app?
Bridge is a real-time voice-to-voice translation engine running 100% locally on your machine with live streaming subtitles and zero-cloud voice cloning playback.

## The angle
Showcase the raw magic of instant, private speech translation: you speak in English, and it translates into Spanish in real time, spoken aloud in your own voice. No cloud subscriptions, no API keys, no latency lag—100% local compute on your GPU.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark, high-contrast title in Space Grotesk emerges against the dark UI backdrop with an active audio pulse:
"Real-time voice translation. Zero cloud. 100% on your machine."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **The Core Engine**: Dual language selection (English ⇌ Spanish) with snappy language swap and live animated dB audio meter.
- **Side-by-Side Live Subtitles**: Real-time speech transcription appearing instantaneously on the left, streaming translated subtitles on the right.
- **Voice Cloning Magic**: "Use my cloned voice" toggles on—hearing your own voice speak a language you don't even know.
- **High-Performance Specs**: 11 languages, 8GB VRAM footprint, complete local privacy.

## Outro / punchline
"Bridge. Speak freely in any language."
Final reveal: GitHub repo link & local setup command `run_bridge.ps1`.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → Key action → Result:
1. **Entry**: Open Bridge UI, select English ⇌ Spanish, verify audio meter responds to your voice.
2. **Key action**: Voice sample active (`🎙️ Use my cloned voice` toggled). Speak into the mic.
3. **Result**: Dual live transcription & instant natural voice playback in your cloned voice with zero network calls.
